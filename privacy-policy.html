Shorts YouTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Shorts YouTube is a personal desktop tool used by the account owner to upload short-form videos to their own TikTok account.

Information we use

When you authorize TikTok, the tool receives the OAuth access and refresh tokens required to call TikTok APIs, plus basic creator information returned by TikTok. Video files and captions are sent to TikTok only when the account owner starts an upload.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored locally on the account owner's computer and are not sold or shared by this tool. We do not operate an analytics or advertising service. TikTok processes data according to its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this tool can be addressed through the TikTok Developer app associated with Shorts YouTube.
